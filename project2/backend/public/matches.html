Matches

Proposed to you

A faculty member can propose that you work with them on one of their opportunities. Each student named answers for themselves: confirm if you intend to take part, or decline if not. The match is confirmed only when every student named has confirmed.

Matches are private: only you, the other students named, and the faculty on the opportunity can see them.

Faculty see you by your email address. Add the name you'd like them to see.

    Your opportunities

    Propose a match by naming the students' Algoma email addresses. Students who haven't signed in yet see it when they do. Each student confirms or declines for themselves; you can withdraw a proposal until everyone has confirmed.

    Once every student named has confirmed, the opportunity is Matched: it leaves student discovery and doesn't expire.
