Who decides whether a result appears publicly?

  • A faculty member who worked on it confirms it. Until then, only the people on it can see it.
  • Once confirmed, it appears in Completed work and on profiles only if every faculty member on it allows public display of their profile.
  • Students are named to visitors only if their own student profile allows it.
  • Each student decides whether it appears on their own profile. That doesn't remove it from the faculty profile or Completed work.
  • Editing a confirmed result takes it out of public view until a faculty member confirms it again.
