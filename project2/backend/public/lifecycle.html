Terms and deadlines

What each state means for students:

  • Open: listed for students until its deadline.
  • Matched: students have confirmed a match, so it's no longer listed and doesn't expire.
  • Expired: the deadline passed without a confirmed match. Nothing is deleted; reopen it with a new deadline, or renew it for a later term.
  • Withdrawn: no longer offered. It can be reopened.
  • Completed: the work is finished. Renew it to offer it again in a later term.
  • Archived: kept for the record only.

Students who open an opportunity that is no longer listed are told which of these applies.
