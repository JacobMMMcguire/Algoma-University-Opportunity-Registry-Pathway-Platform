          If you allow public display:

          • Your faculty profile and your published projects can be seen by anyone, including people who are not signed in.

          If you don't:

          • Your profile and published projects are shown only to people signed in with an Algoma account.
          • You can still use every faculty feature. Declining doesn't limit what you can do.

          Drafts are never shown to anyone else either way. You can change this at any time here, and the change takes effect immediately.
